Period

Long lists over long periods take minutes: backtests read about 2 stocks' candles a second, to stay inside Kite's limits.

Exits

Risk (1R) is entry to SL. With the slow MA as SL, it's entry to the MA at entry, and the trade is closed when a candle closes beyond the MA. Book 100% for a plain target; less, and the rest runs on the trail and exits below. 0% means no target.

What's still open is closed at the close of the last candle before this time; entries after it aren't taken. Not used on daily candles.
