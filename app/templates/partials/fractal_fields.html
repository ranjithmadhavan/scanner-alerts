Watch {% for key, label in fractal_sides.items() %} {% endfor %}

A fractal high being taken reads as a potential sell, a fractal low as a potential buy. Each message gives the target: the nearest unmitigated level on the other side. A gap through a fractal sends nothing but flips it: a low that price gapped below becomes resistance (a potential sell when price comes back up to it), a high gapped above becomes support.
