{# ADX trend's own fields: candle, the ADX/DMI and ATR periods, the three thresholds and the ATR multiple for the SL. #}
Signal candle: all three on its close

Up: +DI over the trending level and −DI under the other; down, the mirror. While all three keep holding, each newer candle becomes the signal candle; the entry is a trade beyond its high (up) or low (down), the SL its other end plus the ATRs above. One trade per trending phase: after an entry, nothing more until the conditions have failed and come back. A phase already under way when the candles start gives no setup.
