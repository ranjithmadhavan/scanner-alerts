One position per stock at a time: an entry while a trade is open is passed over.{% if p.rules.once %} Each crossover gives one entry at most, taken or not (a skipped gap still uses it).{% endif %} A gap entry is filled as chosen above. The SL is checked before the target, so a candle that trades both counts as the SL; on the entry candle, trading beyond the SL counts as stopped (it can't be told whether that came before the entry), while the target counts only on a close beyond it. A gap through either fills at the open. Close-based exits (slow MA, fast-MA trail, square-off) are at that candle's close. Trails and breakeven move at each close. Times are the start of the candle. No costs or slippage. Nothing is saved; the latest run stays here until the app restarts.
